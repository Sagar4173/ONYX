ONYX Logo — 4 Directions

Each approach is ranked by how well it fits the brand name, communicates "premium security intelligence," and scales across the platform. I will give you my definitive recommendation below.

1. Faceted Onyx Gem ← RECOMMENDED

A dark faceted gemstone with cyan-violet light catching the edges

PROS
  • Direct connection to "ONYX = black gem"
  • Facets create natural tech/cyber aesthetic
  • Cyan+violet light maps to brand colors
  • Works at any size (favicon to hero)
  • Completely unique in security space

2. Cybernetic Hex — Evolution of Current

Refined precision hexagon with geometric cutouts and concentric rings

CONS
  • Generic — every security co uses hex
  • No connection to "Onyx" brand name
  • Too similar to current unsatisfying logo

3. Minimalist Letter O / OX Monogram

Perfectly constructed letter O with a precision break, or interlocked O+X

CONS
  • Abstract — no direct meaning
  • Harder to recognize at small sizes
  • Less distinctive among tech logos

4. Quantum Node / Network Intelligence

Orbital paths converging on a central intelligence core — think electron + network

CONS
  • Too abstract — hard to read
  • No connection to "Onyx" brand name
  • Complex at small sizes

My Recommendation: Faceted Onyx Gem

"Onyx" literally means a black gemstone. This is rare brand equity that every great brand leverages (think Apple, Shell, Amazon — their names inform their logos). A faceted gem communicates:

  • Hard • Impenetrable • Secure — core security product values
  • Valuable • Premium • Precious — aligns with "top-tier" positioning
  • Geometric facets — naturally cyber/tech aesthetic without being cliché
  • Cyan-violet light catching the edges — maps perfectly to brand palette

It scales from 16px favicon to full hero, can be rendered in monochrome, and would be completely unique in the security software space where everyone uses shields, hexagons, and locks.
